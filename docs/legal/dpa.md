# Data Processing Addendum

**TEMPLATE. Have a UK solicitor review this before you accept customers or take payment.**

This addendum is for the **hosted** Clausemoor service, where the law firm (or other customer) is the controller and the operator is the processor. It is not the right document for a pure self-hosted install, where the customer operates the software and the operator does not process matter data. It is not legal advice.

**Last updated:** [date]

## 1. Parties and roles

This addendum is between [Operator legal name] ("Processor") and the customer named in the order form ("Controller"). It forms part of the Terms of Service.

The Controller decides why and how personal data in Customer Data is processed. The Processor processes it only to provide the hosted service, and on the Controller's documented instructions, unless the law requires otherwise. If the law requires a disclosure, the Processor tells the Controller first unless the law forbids that.

## 2. Details of processing

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Subject matter | Hosting the Clausemoor workspace |
| Duration | The subscription term, plus the deletion period below |
| Nature and purpose | Store, display, search, and send Customer Data to the language-model providers the Controller enables, so users can review and draft |
| Data subjects | The Controller's users, and people named in documents the Controller uploads |
| Categories | Names, contact details, account identifiers, and the contents of documents and chats the Controller submits |
| Special category data | Not requested. The Controller must not submit it unless the parties agree in writing |

If a subprocessor is outside the UK, the Processor uses a lawful UK transfer tool and will tell the Controller which tool and which country. For EEA controllers, the parties also use the EU SCCs where they are required, with the Processor as the data importer or the relevant module the parties select.

## 6. People and confidentiality

People who can access personal data are under a confidentiality duty.

## 7. Assistance

The Processor helps the Controller, taking into account the nature of the processing, with data-subject requests, security, breach notices, and data-protection impact assessments that relate to the service. The Controller pays for help that goes beyond what the product already provides, at the agreed support rate.

## 8. Breaches

The Processor tells the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting Customer Data, and provides the information the Controller reasonably needs to meet its own notification duties.

## 9. Deletion and return

At the end of the service, the Controller may export Customer Data for [30] days. The Processor then deletes it from the live hosted service, unless the law requires retention. Backups age out on the cycle described in the security annex.

## 10. Audits

The Processor provides the information reasonably needed to show compliance. An on-site audit is limited to once a year, on reasonable notice, unless a regulator or a confirmed breach requires more. Audits are subject to confidentiality and must not compromise other customers.
